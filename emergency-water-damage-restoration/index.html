Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
