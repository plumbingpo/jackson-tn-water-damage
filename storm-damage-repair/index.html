Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
